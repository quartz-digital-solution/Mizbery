Wondering

Here are a few helpful answers before we get in touch.

Can I enquire about bulk quantities? +

Yes. Use the enquiry form and share the estimated quantity, preferred bottle format and delivery location so the team can review your requirement.

Which bottle sizes can I ask about? +

You can enquire about 500 ml bottles, 1 litre bottles and larger-order requirements. Confirm the currently available formats and packaging when you contact the team.

Can I ask about a recurring supply? +

Yes. Mention that you need a recurring supply and include your location and approximate order frequency.

How do I send an enquiry? +

Fill in the form below. When Mizbery's WhatsApp or email is configured, the website prepares a message for that contact. Until then, you can copy the enquiry text.
